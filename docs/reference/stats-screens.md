# Stats screens — reference

Added in v0.28.0 (Plan AA; spec `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-15-stats-analysis-design.md` §9.1–§9.2, amended in §18); the team dashboard in v0.29.0 (Plan AB; §9.3, amended in §19); the player dashboard in v0.30.0 (Plan AC; §9.4, amended in §21); Explore's Compare and Trends in v0.34.0 (Plan AD1; §9.5, amended in §22) and its chart explorer in v0.35.0 (Plan AD2; §23); Games in v0.36.0 (Plan AE; §9.5, amended in §24). Companion Stats is the app's second product: its own shell and cobalt palette (design handoff 2026-10-16, "Companion Stats"), reachable from the Fantasy nav ("Stats ↗"), and where the app opens when no league is imported. Every number comes from the query engine (`stats.query`, data reference: `docs/reference/stats-database.md`).

## Shell

- **Nav:** Leaderboards · Players (the player dashboard) · Teams (the team dashboard) · Explore (Chart, Compare, Trends) · Games (a week's games: previews and box scores) · Glossary; "Data through 2026 week 6 · updated 2 h ago" from `stats.status()` ("updating…" while a refresh runs); "Fantasy ↗" (Setup without a league); the theme toggle.
- **Scope** (every screen, kept for the session): season (2019 → latest), Regular season / Postseason / Both, the regular season's week range, and the fantasy scoring (My league once a league is imported, else PPR, Half PPR, Standard).
- A finished rebuild (`stats:changed`) re-reads the status and the catalogue and runs every query on screen again.

## Leaderboards (home)

Eight boards, the top six of each: QB passing yards and yards per attempt, RB rushing yards and touches, WR receiving yards and target share, TE receiving yards, team takeaways (`def_turnovers`). A row needs the metric's own qualification and half the regular-season games in range (one in the postseason). Totals or per game. With a league, "My players" marks my players with an amber dot and adds my best one under a dashed line when he ranks outside the top six. "All →" opens the full leaderboard sorted by that metric; a row opens that player's (or team's) dashboard. Saved views are listed above the boards.

## Players and Teams

The Ledger table: one tab per position group (All offense, QB, RB, WR, TE, K, P, DL, LB, DB) or team unit (Offense, Defense, Ratings & odds), each with default columns in named groups (`src/renderer/src/lib/stats/leaderboard.ts`).

| Shown            | From                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            |
| ---------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Value            | The metric's value after Totals / Per game (`perGame` divides counts by games)                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  |
| Shading          | Its percentile among the rows the tab qualifies (NQ below), of the same position, and above the metric's own sample (teams: the league): ≥ 90 / 75 / 60 green, ≤ 10 / 25 / 40 red; neutral metrics (pass rate, pace) unshaded                                                                                                                                   |
| Hover on a value | Percentile, rank, the sample in words (`MetricInfo.sampleLabel`), "below the qualifying sample", "estimated"                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    |
| `est`            | Routes and the shares built on them in the current season (participation is published after the season)                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         |
| Column head      | Short label, reliability marker (three bars: high, medium, low year-to-year stability); hover: the catalogue entry (description, family, positions, sample and minimum, seasons, sources, reliability)                                                                                                                                                          |
| G                | The row's games                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 |
| NQ               | Below the tab's qualifier: QB dropbacks (`qb_epa_db`, 200), RB carries (`carry_share`, 50), WR / TE targets (`target_share`, 30), K field-goal attempts (20), P punts (30), defenders' defensive snaps (200), All offense's offensive snaps (200); each per 17 games, scaled to the weeks in range. "Qualified only" (default) hides them. Teams always qualify |
| Owner mark       | With a league: amber dot for my players (my defense), a hollow ring for another team's, its name on hover                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       |

- **A team row** (on the team leaderboard, or a home board's) opens that team's dashboard. The team leaderboard is under the dashboard's "Team leaderboards →" and the home's "All →".
- **A player row** (on a player leaderboard, a home board, or a team dashboard's tables) opens that player's dashboard. The player leaderboards are under the dashboard's "Player leaderboards →" (at his position, his row selected) and the home's "All →".
- **Filters:** Totals / Per game, Qualified only, team (players), with a league All / My team / Free agents, search by name. Ownership and search filter the engine's rows, so percentiles stay among the whole position.
- **Columns:** "Columns: this tab" or one family's core metrics on the tab (Traditional, Advanced, Next Gen, Charting, Predictive; extended ones fill in to 12); the column picker adds any core or extended metric of the tab (at most 40) or removes one; Reset.
- **Sort:** a column head (best first, then the other way), or the name. The engine sorts; the table shows 50 rows at a time.
- **Views:** a named board saved in `companion.db` (`stats_views`): entity, tab, columns, sort, per game, qualified only, team, owner filter. The season, weeks and scoring stay the screen's. Saving under an existing name (any case) replaces it.
- **CSV:** every row the filters keep (not only the 50 shown): rank, name, team, position and gsis id (players), games, then each column unformatted, percentages in percent. Saved where the dialog says (Downloads by default), UTF-8 with a byte-order mark so Excel reads accents.

## Team dashboard

Teams in the nav opens it on the team viewed last; the first time, on the team of my top fantasy scorer in range (with a league), else ARI. ‹ › and the select move between the 32 teams.

- **Header** (every tab; handoff "8. Team"): the team's name, its place in the division (regular season: win %, then point margin), the abbreviation, four tiles (record, points and points allowed per game, the margin per game in its tone), a results strip with one card per week of the range (W/L and the score, byes, postseason rounds WC / DIV / CONF / SB), and the scope bar; Compare (opens Explore › Compare with the team and the two teams nearest it in Elo). With a league, who rosters the team's defense.
- **Stat lines** (Offense, Defense, Special teams, Situational, Tendencies, Forecast, the Overview's ratings): the value; a percentile pill and the rank among the teams that qualify ("3rd of 32", ties "T-3rd" with their percentile at the middle); the league's mean; the catalogue entry and reliability on the label. A metric with no better side (pass rate, pace, blitz rate) reads "11th highest" and stays grey. A line the season lacks says why ("Not published for 2026 yet.", "Charted from 2022."). Each line comes from one query over every team, one more per split. Hovering a line (or focusing it) shows a trend icon at its left: Explore › Trends on that metric for the team. Split lines have none.

| Tab           | Shows                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   |
| ------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Overview      | Who gets the targets and the carries (share of the team's total in range, bars against the leader, FP/G under the scoring), who throws; the next game (Elo and the market's win probability and spread, total, weather); ratings; EPA per play by week                  |
| Offense       | Efficiency, passing, rushing (line yards, stuffs), red zone (EPA and success inside the 20) and third / fourth down, ball security                                                                                                                                      |
| Defense       | Efficiency allowed, pass rush (pressure through the prior season, sacks, blitz, rushers), coverage (man rate, EPA in man and zone, through the prior season), run defense, havoc and red zone, fantasy points allowed by position under the scoring                     |

Charts: one line per series (cobalt, teal, magenta, each with its own marker: circle, square, diamond, so the lines read apart without colour), gaps for byes, end labels, and a hover that lists every series that week.

## Player dashboard

Players in the nav opens it on the player viewed last; the first time, on my top fantasy scorer in range (with a league, once the rosters have loaded), else the leading WR by receiving yards. A Fantasy player file's "Full stats ↗" opens it too (a team defense opens its team dashboard).

- **Rail** (handoff "6. Player profile"): the nine positions (QB, RB, WR, TE, K, P, DL, LB, DB), a search, a rank-by metric (the position leaderboard's columns), and the players the position's leaderboard qualifies, best first, the value in a chip shaded by its percentile. The player shown stays listed below the qualifier ("NQ"). ↑ / ↓ or J / K move the selection; picking a position opens its leader; 25 rows, then "Show 25 more".
- **Header** (every tab): Sleeper's headshot (initials when it does not load), the position chip, the team (opens its dashboard), games in range, "Not qualified", the status tag, the fantasy rank under the scoring ("WR4 in PPR", fantasy positions); the name he goes by (first, then last); number, his age that season, season of his career, draft, college, size; the scope bar; Compare (opens Explore › Compare with him and his position's top two); with a league and a fantasy position, the owner chip ("On your fantasy team", "Rostered by …", "Free agent in your league") that opens his Fantasy file.
- **Status tag:** the latest roster week's reserve list (IR, PUP, NFI, SUS, RES) or the latest week's injury report (OUT, D, Q; DNP or LP in practice before a game status), the latest season only. Hover: the status, the injury and the week.
- **Stat lines** (as the team dashboard's): value, percentile pill and rank among his position's players the leaderboard qualifies who also meet the metric's own sample ("4th of 110"), the qualified players' mean ("Lg"). Below the qualifier, values without ranks. An empty line says why: "Not published for 2026 yet.", "Charted from 2022.", "No plays in this split.", "No NFL Next Gen Stats value for him." (NGS and ESPN QBR publish players over their own minimums). A line's trend icon (on hover or focus) opens Explore › Trends on that metric for him.

## Explore

Explore in the nav opens the tab used last (Chart the first time). Its sub-tabs sit at the left of the bar, the scope bar at its right: Chart, Compare, Trends. Each tab keeps its players, teams and choices for the session; Back returns to the page before Explore.

### Chart

Any metric against any other (handoff "15. Chart explorer"): players at one position (the nine of the rail) or the 32 teams.

- **Bar:** Players / Teams, the position, Totals / Per game (players).
- **Controls (left column):** presets (a volume against an efficiency per position, WR "Volume vs efficiency" first; teams "Offense vs defense EPA" and "Pass vs rush EPA"); the Y and X axes, every metric with a season grain by family, "↓" on a lower-is-better one, and a button to swap them; size by and colour by a third metric's percentile (metrics with a better side), with the colour key; a team, qualified only, my players and a search; the qualifying rule and how many qualify on both axes.
- **Scatter:** a dot per row (the qualified only, by default), up and right always better (a lower-is-better axis runs reversed), the qualified means dashed with the four corners in words. Rings: my players amber, a search's finds ink, the hovered cobalt, the picks in the series colours. Labels: the picks and finds, mine, then the six players farthest from the crowd, or every team; none overlaps. Hover: the name, team and position, each metric's value and percentile chip, games and the sample, "Click to open". A click opens the dashboard; shift-click picks up to three, and "Compare these" opens Compare with them.
- **Under it:** r as plotted (positive is better on both), how many are shown, the means; a table (#, name, team, each metric with its percentile chip and "est.") sorted by a column's percentile, best first (again for worst first), or by name; the engine's notes.
- **States:** "Too few qualifiers to chart" under 10 dots (the postseason at TE: 6), with "Use weeks 1–7" and "Show all 31 TEs" where they help; "Pressure rate is not published for 2026 yet." with "Use 2025".
- Percentiles are among the position's qualified players who meet each metric's own sample (Compare's pool).

### Compare

Three slots side by side (handoff "7. Compare"): players at one position (the nine of the rail) or teams.

- **Cards:** the series marker (--s1 circle, --s2 square, --s3 diamond), × to remove one (two must stay). Players: team, games, NQ below the position's qualifier, the name (opens his dashboard), "Leads n of m", a swap select (my players first, "· your team", then the position's qualified players best first, then the rest). Teams: record and division place, the abbreviation and name (opens the team dashboard), Elo, "Leads n of m", a swap select of the 32. An open slot is a dashed card, "Add a third player…".
- **Defaults:** players, the position's top three by its leaderboard's sort, my best player at the position in slot 3 when none of mine is there; teams, the top three by Elo. From a dashboard's Compare: that player and the position's top two, or that team and the two teams nearest it in Elo. The slots stay when the range changes.
- **Rows:** players, the position leaderboard's groups (WR: Usage, Receiving, Efficiency, Fantasy); teams, Offense (EPA per play, success rate, explosive-play rate, EPA per dropback, EPA per carry, sack rate), Defense (the same allowed; pressure and sack rate as the defense's own), Special teams (FG over expected, net punting), Situational (third down and red-zone TD rate, both sides). Each cell: the value (counts per game or totals for players), the handoff's pill-dot bar of its percentile and its rank ("#4") among the position's qualified players who meet the metric's sample, or the 32. The best value of a row is bold (none on a tie between all three, or a metric with no better side). A row the season lacks says why ("Not published for 2026 yet.": pressure rate is participation data).
- **Chart:** fantasy points by week (players at positions without fantasy points: their first tile, sacks for DL), or a team's EPA per play by week, offense or defense, with the median of the pool each week dashed. Lower-is-better runs upside down ("Flipped so up is better").

### Trends

A metric by season, 2019 → now, for a player or a team, and up to two more of the same position (or teams) for context.

- **Bar:** Players / Teams, the position (players), a search (everyone with a row at the position since 2019, or the 32 teams), the metric (every core and extended metric with a season grain, by family), Totals / Per game for a count.
- **Header:** the position chip, the name, the team now, his age, his season of the league, his teams ("NYJ 2021–23, BUF since 2024"); a pill per series, × on a comparison; "+ Add a comparison" (a search).
- **Chart:** the metric each season of his span (his first season to his last; a season without a row stays on the axis). The position's qualified median dashed over its 25th–75th percentile band; his line (a season below the qualifier hollow) and the comparisons (squares, diamonds). Under each season: his age, and his team when it changed (a trade: "NYJ → LAR"). Hover: the season, team, value, "12th of 110 · 89th percentile" or "Not qualified", the sample. Coverage notes beside the title: "Charted from 2022.", "Next Gen Stats from 2019.", "Not published for 2026 yet.", "Estimated for 2026.".
- **Table:** newest first: season, team (teams: none), age, games, the value ("not charted" before the metric starts, "est." when estimated), the change from the season before in the tone of the metric's better side (points for a rate in percent), the percentile chip (NQ), rank, sample. A season below the qualifier is faded.
- **Career at a glance:** six core metrics of the position (WR: target share, air-yards share, YPRR, separation, route participation, fantasy points per game; teams: EPA per play, success rate, explosive-play rate, EPA per dropback, EPA per carry, red-zone TD rate): the last value ("(2025)" when it is not this season's), its percentile chip, a note, a sparkline of the percentile each season. Clicking one charts it.
- **One season:** "Trends need two seasons; he has one." with that season's value and rank.
- Every season is ranked among that season's pool: one query over the position by season, each season qualified against its own weeks (the season in progress too).

## Games

A season's games week by week (handoff "9. Game", the "Stats Games" canvas): the season is the shared scope's, the week the screen's own (the next game's week at first, else the last played; kept for the session).

- **Rail (240px):** Season and Week (the postseason by round), "14 GAMES · 4 BYES"; each game away over home with the scores (the winner bold) or, before it, the kickoff ("Sun 1:00 PM", the machine's time zone) and the home team's line ("−3.5"). Games with my fantasy players carry the amber dot and come first, then by kickoff. The teams on a bye under the list.
- **Bar:** Preview · Box score (the box score once the game is final; a final game opens on it), what the team stats cover ("Team stats: weeks 1–7", "…, before the game"), the fantasy scoring.
- **Header:** each team (a click opens its dashboard) with its record, Elo and last four results before the game, and its Elo win probability, or its score once final; the kickoff or "Final · Week 7", indoors or outdoors.
- **The forecast** ("before kickoff" for a final game): Elo's and the market's win probability as bars, whether they agree ("Elo likes GB more than the market does (67% against 62%)."), and the result against the favorite; the spread (the market's, Elo's under it), the total and each team's implied points; the conditions (indoors, or the temperature and wind; nflverse fills them in late: none since 2026 week 5).
- **Phase by phase:** each offense against the other defense over the weeks before the game (week 1: the season before; a postseason game: its regular season; 2019 week 1 has nothing before it and says so). Overall (adjusted EPA, success rate, explosive-play rate), Passing (EPA and success per dropback), Rushing (EPA per carry, stuff rate), Protection vs pass rush (sack rate, pressure rate), Finishing (red-zone TD rate, third-down %). Each side's value and its rank among the 32 on its own better side, in a chip shaded by percentile; a bar from the centre leans toward the side with the edge (the offense's percentile minus the defense's, half the width at 100 points); the larger value of the edge in bold; the biggest edge in words. A metric the season lacks: "Not published for 2026 yet.".
- **Key matchups:** each team's QB, RB, two WRs and TE by fantasy points before the game, among the players of its last game (a final game: its box score), with points per game and their rank at the position; the other defense's rank against the position in fantasy points allowed per game (1st the toughest; 11th or better red, 22nd or worse green); with a league, my players marked and Sleeper's projection for the week (a final game: the points each scored). Then the other defense's leading pass rusher (sacks, then QB hits) against the line's sack rate. A row opens the player's dashboard.
- **Box score:** the top five fantasy performances, then per team passing, rushing and receiving (the fantasy points last), the kicker's field goals and the defense's sacks, takeaways and touchdowns (its defenders' sums). A row opens the player's dashboard.
- The preview reads the matchup as three narrow queries at once (offenses, defenses, fantasy points allowed), and the box score as three (passing; rushing and receiving; kickers and defenders): DuckDB ranks each metric in its own window step, so one wide query is slower.

## Glossary

Every core and extended metric, players or teams, by family or search, with the same entry as the column hover.

## IPC

`stats.games({ season, team })` for the dashboard's header, next game and schedule (`{ season }` for a player's log and Games, each game with its `kickoff`); Fantasy's `players.week({ season, week })` for Sleeper's projections in Games; `stats.player({ player })` for a player's bio and status; `stats.outlook({ player, season, scoring })` for his Forecast; `stats.link({ sleeper } | { gsis })` (the same player in both products, from `player_ids`); `stats.ownership()` (the league's rosters keyed by gsis id and nflverse team code, from `player_ids`), `stats.views()`, `stats.saveView(name, board)`, `stats.deleteView(id)`, `stats.exportCsv(fileName, csv)`; and Plan Y's `stats.query`, `stats.catalogue`, `stats.status`, `stats.onChanged`.

## Speed

Measured on the live database (2026 week 6): the home's eight boards on screen in about 90 ms, a leaderboard tab 100–210 ms from click to table, a sort 120 ms, a 40-column query 265 ms. The team dashboard: Teams 140 ms from click to the Overview, a tab 65–150 ms, Situational (eleven queries) 275 ms. The player dashboard: Players 230–290 ms from click to the Overview, a tab 70–135 ms, Splits 265–480 ms, a rail position 135–275 ms. Explore (2026 week 7): the chart on screen 60–106 ms from click, a position 41–105 ms, Teams 28–70 ms; Compare on screen 160–205 ms from click, a position or Players / Teams 135–185 ms; the Trends tab 245–255 ms, a new metric 220–290 ms. Games (2026 week 7 data): on screen 219–225 ms from click (342 ms the first time after launch), another game 37–42 ms, a final week's box score 141–156 ms, the Preview tab 29–44 ms; in the worker, a preview's queries at once 84–204 ms, a box score's 64–132 ms.
